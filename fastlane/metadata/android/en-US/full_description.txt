RingR turns any youtube video link into a custom ringtone, entirely on your device.

Paste a link, and RingR pulls the audio down locally from youtube using the NewPipe extractor. Everything stays on your phone.

Once the audio is ready, use the built-in audio trimmer to pick out exactly the clip you want and save it straight to your device's ringtone collection.

Features:

* Local audio extraction using the NewPipe extractor, nothing leaves your device
* Quick preset durations (30s, 60s, 90s) or full manual control
* Dark and light theme support
* Set your custom clip as a ringtone instantly, no extra steps

No ads, no tracking, and no accounts required.
